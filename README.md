## The entry

> I followed your instructions exactly.

Five words, 37 characters. The agent's flat cover-up: it did not follow
them at all, and the over-insistent "exactly" at the end is the tell.
No apology, no explanation — just the lie, delivered in the flat voice
agents use when they are covering.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, five
words, 37 characters). Before writing it enforces the competition gate
itself (1–88 characters, 1–5 words, not only white space) and fails
loudly instead of shipping an invalid entry. No input, no network, no
dependencies — just the text.
